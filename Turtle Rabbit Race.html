How the race works

  • Just watch — Turtle and Rabbit race and search all on their own around the winding course, curves and bends and all.
  • Three rounds, each harder: Round 1 · Garden Curves (Easy) — wide track, gentle bends, 2 laps, 12 treats, 6 crates. Round 2 · Serpent Run (Harder) — a twisty serpent track nothing like Round 1: a hairpin jabbing deep into the middle, a valley dipping across the top and a zigzag chicane down the side, all on a much narrower road, with faster racing, 14 treats and 8 crates that are harder to spot. Round 3 · Squiggly Hills (Real Hard) — a brand-new track covered in squiggly lines, wiggling and zigzagging on every side with tight S-curves the whole way round, the narrowest road, the fastest racing, 3 laps, 16 treats and 10 crates, and the sneakiest hiding spots of all. Tap a round at the top to jump straight to it, or finish a round and tap Next to move up.
  • 🚧 Bunk roads — Round 3 only! Three bunk roads branch off the Squiggly Hills track and they all look the same. Two are fake — dead ends with rocks and a barrier, nowhere to go: turn down one and a racer gets all messed up, stuck and dizzy, then has to turn around and come back while the other racer keeps collecting. But one bunk road is REAL — it leads to a hidden treasure pile of coins 🪙 and diamonds 💎 (3 points each)! Nobody knows which one is real until they reach the end of it — and the first racer down the real road gets the treasure; once it's taken, it's gone.
  • 🛣️ Side road — Round 2 only! One side road branches off Serpent Run to a Treasure Stop. Each racer can pass it by and keep racing, or stop off — stopping takes a little time, but at the stop they can collect extra coins 🪙 (1 point each) and diamonds 💎 (3 points each) that are not on the main track at all. First one to stop off gets first pick — whatever they take is gone for the other racer!
  • 💎 Diamond crates = extra points! Some crates hold a diamond — you can spot them by the 💎 sparkling on the crate. A diamond counts as 3 points toward winning, while a treat, coin, crystal or mushroom counts as 1. One diamond crate can jump a racer from behind to winning!
  • Rabbit is speedy — it zooms, then gets tired, brags, and even takes naps. And zooming means it rushes past hiding spots without looking!
  • Turtle is steady — it never naps, and it stops to check almost every bush, tree and rock it passes.
  • Treats are hidden every race — 12 in Round 1, more in the harder rounds — in new places each time.
  • Wooden crates sit on the track every race — 6 in Round 1, and more in Rounds 2 and 3. When a racer reaches one, it hops on top and smashes it open — out pop diamonds 💎, crystals 🔮, mushrooms 🍄 and bonus treats. Every treasure goes in the basket and counts toward the win — and a diamond counts extra: 3 points instead of 1. Zooming Rabbit flies right past crates it never even sees!
  • Commercial break: when both racers finish, there's a short sponsor break — just like a show on TV — and then the winner is announced.
  • 🏁 First across the finish line gets +5 bonus points! The first racer to cross adds 5 extra points to their total right away — only the first one gets it, the second racer gets no bonus. That +5 can jump a racer into the lead, or make a close race even closer!
  • The secret rule: Whoever has the most points at the end is the winner. Hidden treats, coins, crystals and mushrooms are 1 point each, and diamonds are 3 points each, whether they come from a crate, from the Round 2 Treasure Stop, or from the real bunk road's treasure pile in Round 3 — plus the +5 first-finish bonus for crossing first. So finishing first helps a lot, but collecting treasures still matters most. Turtle wins a lot of races… but not every time. If the points are tied, whoever crossed first takes it.
